Retail Inventory Availability and Stockout Risk Analysis Dashboard

Business Objective : 
Ensure continuous product availability by identifying inventory shortages, monitoring stockout risks and 
preventing potential revenue losses.

Business Problems :
1. How much inventory is actually available?
2. Which products are out of stock?
3. Which products are approaching stockout?
4. How many days will the current inventory last?
5. Which categories and warehouses faces highest stockout risks?
6. Which Products may runout before supplier delivery?
7. How much potential revenue is at risk due to insufficient inventory?



Datasets(.csv):
	1. Product_Master
	2. Warehouse_Master
	3. Supplier_Master
	4. Inventory_snapshot --> Inventory availability at different timestamp
	5. sales_transactions --> day-to-day sales
	6. purchase_orders --> Products ordered to supplier to replenish inventory

4. Inventory_Snapshot Table
	i. Trim, clean and make uppercase product_id, warehouse_id columns.
	ii. Delete duplicate records or duplicate {snapshot_date, product_id, warehouse_id} combination.
	iii. Opening_stock + received_stock - units_sold + stock_adjustment = closing_stock.
	iv. Closing_stock - reserved_stock - damaged_stock = available_stock
	v. Make all null stock_adjustment_units as 0. (inc. or dec. in stock_adjustment occur from stock_corrections, counting_errors, returns, write-offs etc)

	vii. Bring avg_lead_time_days from supplier_table to inventory_snapshot through product_table Using Merge_queries.
		match product_id from inventory to product_id from product_table. match primary_supplier_id from product_table to supplier_table. take avg_lead_time_days from supplier table and put in into inventory table.
	viii. Create a new column called "Stock_Risk_Level" and put the formula 
		if [available_stock]<0 --> Out of stock, 
		if (days_of_cover < supplier_delivery_time) or (available_quantity < reorder_point_units) --> High
		if (days_of_cover = supplier_delivery_time) or (available_quantity = reorder_point_units) --> Moderate
		else --> Low
	ix. Put Selling_price_INR from product_table to inventory_table through merge_queries.
	x. Create another custom column called "Revenue_at_risk" and use the formula max(0,(avg_lead_time_days-days_of_cover))*(units_sold/30)*selling_price
